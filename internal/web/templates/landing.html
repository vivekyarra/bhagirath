Dirty water complaints that cannot be closed without proof.

In Indore's Bhagirathpura, residents complained about dirty water for months before an outbreak killed at least 16 people. The complaints, the water tests and the illness signals existed — in three places nobody connected. Bhagirath connects them, and keeps following up with officials until residents confirm the fix.
